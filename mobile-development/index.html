Our custom app development services

From concept to launch and beyond, we bring expertise to every phase of the mobile app lifecycle.

Discover our comprehensive package of custom mobile app development services. Choose what fits or go all-in to ensure your app earns five-star ratings.

Discovery: Laying the foundation for your mobile app

The journey to a successful mobile app starts here. Our discovery phase is designed to uncover your vision, refine your ideas, and set the stage for development. Here’s how we do it:

Requirement analysis: We're eager to know as much as possible about your expectations and app vision: target audience, use cases, features, long-term goals, timelines, and budget. The more we know, the faster we can kick off development. 

Got open questions? Our experts are ready to guide you with professional advice and industry best practices.

Market research: We can help you better understand competitors in your niche — what features their apps offer, what hooks their target audience — and suggest a differentiation strategy. 

Feature elicitation: Our specialists define your app's essential features and create simple mockups and wireframes to visualize its look and feel.

Feasibility analysis: Right from the start, we assess whether your project can be delivered as planned — on time, within budget, and with the features you want. If any challenges arise, we’ll identify them early and work with you to adjust the plan for success.

Strategic planning: Setting the stage for well-oiled development

You want your mobile app development journey to be smooth and predictable. So do we. That’s why we thoroughly plan every step, leaving no detail to chance. 

Mobile app shaping: We turn everything uncovered during the discovery phase into an SRS (software requirements specification). By prioritizing features for your MVP (minimum viable product), we help you balance essential functionality and your time-to-market goals.

Our experts recommend a mobile platform and technology stack and design the app architecture. 

Project roadmap: With the big picture in place, we craft a detailed project plan that breaks everything down into actionable steps. This roadmap includes:

  • Milestones to track progress
  • Timelines to stay on schedule
  • Resource and role allocation
  • Detailed cost estimates are to be kept within your budget.

 

Design: Crafting the look and feel of your app

Great UI/UX design captivates users — often within seconds. That’s why we focus on creating intuitive designs that win hearts at first glance.

Prototyping: Prototyping offers a sneak peek into your app’s functionality and flow, helping refine complex projects before committing to a full-scale design. For small-scale apps or when iterative development is planned, we can skip prototyping to save time while maintaining quality.

UI/UX: We map out user journeys to uncover how they’ll navigate your app, what excites them, and where potential friction points might arise. The result? An intuitive, seamless experience free from bottlenecks and hurdles — designed to bring delight with every tap, swipe, and scroll.

User testing: Real users, real insights. We invite representatives of your target audience to walk through specific scenarios in your app. This critical step helps us pinpoint design flaws and refine every element, ensuring your app feels as smooth and natural as it looks.

Development: Building the face and brain of your app

We bring your app to life with attention to detail, staying aligned with your vision and focused on delivering excellence.

Frontend: We build responsive, visually captivating interfaces that adapt seamlessly across devices. Lightweight designs, smooth animations, and fast-loading screens ensure a flawless user experience, optimized for battery efficiency and one-handed comfort.

Backend: We build secure servers, robust databases, and the logic that drives your app’s features, ensuring seamless, lightning-fast data flow and reliability at every step.

Testing: Ensuring quality that wows the world

Quality is everything, so we offer comprehensive mobile testing services to leave no aspect untested and no detail unchecked.

Functionality testing: From functionality and usability to performance and compatibility, our QA team ensures your app delivers on every promise — no glitches, no surprises.

Usability testing: We test app navigation, design, and flow to ensure users can achieve their goals without frustration. 

Performance testing: Our QA team ensures your app can handle the pressure — be it heavy traffic, spotty connectivity, or demanding usage scenarios.

Compatibility testing: We test your app across a range of phones, tablets, and OS versions to make sure it shines everywhere.

Launch and publishing: Going live

Your app is polished and ready for the big stage. Here’s what happens next:

Preparation for submission: We set up your Apple or Google developer account and ensure your app meets all App Store or Google Play Store guidelines.

App publishing: From crafting a compelling app page with all the essential details and engaging visuals to uploading your app, submitting it for review, and finally hitting publish — we handle it all so you can focus on celebrating your launch.

Flexible cooperation models

Every business is unique, and so are the challenges it faces. Whether you're looking for complete development support or just an extra boost for your team, we offer an engagement model tailored for you.

01

When to choose outsourcing?

You need end-to-end mobile app development with minimal involvement. Outsourcing is ideal if you prefer focusing on your core business while leaving the heavy lifting to a third party.

As your trusted outsourcing partner, we'll handle daily workflows, manage the team, monitor budget adherence and deadlines, and plan and deliver your mobile app.

You’ll stay in control with regular syncs with our project manager and clear milestone and KPI reports.

02

When to choose a dedicated team?

You have no in-house experts to execute your app vision and hiring isn’t part of the long-term plan. A dedicated team is a perfect scenario as it gives you full control over development workflows.

Engage the team of Ventioneers — proven professionals who have delivered dozens of mobile projects. The team typically includes roles like a mobile developer, a UI/UX designer, a QA specialist, and a DevOps engineer, but it can be adjusted or scaled to match your specific business needs.

03

When to choose staff augmentation?

You’ve got the necessary talent and streamlined workflows, but what you need is an extra edge — and fast. Whether adding developers to accelerate delivery or bringing in AI and blockchain experts to close skills gaps, staff augmentation gets you there without delay.

With Vention, you’ll find the right fit every time. Our 3,000+ professionals are top-tier domain experts and specialists in cutting-edge technologies, ready to hit the ground running and deliver results.

FAQs

How does cooperation with Vention work?

We make onboarding seamless and fast:

  • Assess: Within hours, we’ll connect to understand your goals and challenges, identifying the developers best suited to your needs.
  • Screen: You’ll receive CVs of top candidates from our expert roster for your review.
  • Select: We preselect candidates, and you can interview them to choose the perfect match for your team.
  • Start: A kickoff meeting ensures your project gets up and running without delay.

How much does it cost to build a mobile app?

The cost depends on the development approach (custom vs. general), app feature complexity, UX requirements, etc. Please contact our engagement team directly or use our price calculator to get a tailored cost estimate.

How long does it take to develop a mobile app?

It all comes down to the app’s complexity, planned features, and chosen development approach. While timelines vary, a simple app can take around three to six months to develop, whereas a complex app with advanced features may require nine to 12 months or more. Share your app vision with us, and we’ll provide a clear time estimate.

What team do you need to build a mobile app?

A typical mobile app development team includes a project manager, business analyst, UX/UI designer, frontend developer, backend developer, mobile app developer (iOS, Android, or cross-platform), quality assurance engineer, security specialist, and DevOps engineer.

How will you ensure the app is secure?

We ensure app security through advanced encryption, role-based access controls, and secure coding practices to eliminate vulnerabilities.
